The Place

Turn off the road
and the noise stops

A wooden cabin with the lights on. Thatched huts, tables under the trees, open field running out to the treeline. There is a horse, a buffalo farm along the fence, and water beyond it.

In the evening the string lights come on, someone picks up a guitar, and nobody is in a hurry to leave.

No park permit. No gate fee. No jeep to book.You are three kilometres short of the Sauraha gate and none of that applies here.

The Grounds

What is actually here

A horse

Guests meet the horse before they meet the menu. It is the first thing people mention afterwards.

Field to the treeline

Grass, saplings and space, running out to standing forest. Buffalo farm along the fence, water beyond it.

The machan

A raised thatched platform, the way they are built across the Terai. Best seat on the property when the sun drops.

Fire and string lights

After dark the lights come on across the huts and the lane, and the fire gets going.

Somebody playing

A guitar under the thatch, unamplified, no stage. It happens because people bring instruments.

Room to park

Off the road, room for cars and a line of bikes. Riding groups can pull in together.

Riders

The motorcycle on our badge
is not decoration

Chitwan sits in the middle of every good loop in this country — Kathmandu down through Mugling, across to Pokhara, out to Lumbini and back.

We are a few minutes off that line. Somewhere to put the bikes, food that does not take an hour, ground to pitch a tent on and a fire to sit around after. Club runs, weekend groups, solo riders passing through.
